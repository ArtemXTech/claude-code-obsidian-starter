# Submersive

Premium immersive bathhouse at 901 Barton Springs, Austin TX. A novel category — part thermal bathhouse, part immersive art experience, part neuroaesthetic wellness destination. 25,000 sq ft buildout. Soft open March 2027.

## Positioning

**FROM** "Trippy Spa of the Future" **TO** science-based immersive wellness (neuroaesthetics anchor).

**Tone:** Premium, knowledge-grounded, aspirational but accessible.

**Content pillars:** Submersive vision / Historical bathing cultures / Corvas's journey / Scientific backing.

**Avoid:** Psychedelic framing, "trippy" language, hype-driven language.

## Business Model (Three Layers)

1. **Layer 1:** Bathhouse (thermal wellness core)
2. **Layer 2:** Biometrics (personalization data)
3. **Layer 3:** Adaptive environments (AI-driven experiences)

## Membership Model (10-Year Projections)

Membership price: **$299/yr**. Three scenarios modeled:

| Scenario | Penetration | Retention | Y1 Revenue | Y5 Revenue | Y10 Revenue |

| Conservative | 3% | 50% | $819K | $2.0M | $2.3M |
| Base | 5% | 55% | $1.4M | $3.3M | $3.5M |

~91K guests/year Y1, declining to ~68K Y10. Entries/guest grow from 1.1 to 2.5 over 10 years (~100K→170K annual entries).

Market benchmarks: 2-4% penetration early, 7-10% plateau for experience venues, 50-60% retention reasonable, 80% aggressive (Soho House tier).

Note: Model is additive — doesn't factor in cannibalization or breakage. Excludes F&B/merch revenue from members.
