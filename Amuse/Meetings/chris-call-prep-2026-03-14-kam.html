Ticketing Platform Options

New scope

Submersive's 4 Booking Needs

NeedDescriptionChallenge
MembershipsRecurring billing, perks, priority accessMust own the CRM relationship
Day PassesTimed entry, walk-up + online, capacity mgmtReal-time zone-level capacity
EventsSound baths, DJ nights, collaborationsOverlay on regular operations
DiscoveryReaching new audiences at scaleMarketing reach vs. data ownership

Platform Landscape

ROLLER
Attractions + memberships + timed entry + POS
Recommended
Purpose-built for venues like Submersive. Handles memberships, timed entry, capacity by zone, POS, waivers. You own the guest relationship.
Fever
Discovery marketplace + GA ticketing
Distribution
200M+ user base for discovery. Sells INTO your capacity system. Handles general admission only. Founding memberships must NOT live here.
Mindbody
Wellness/fitness booking + business mgmt
Alternative
If wellness/spa framing matters more than attraction framing. Handles memberships, scheduling, POS. Consumer marketplace. $99-399+/mo.
Tock / Eventbrite / DICE
Various
Not recommended
Tock: restaurant DNA, no memberships. Eventbrite: mass-market brand, high fees. DICE: now owned by Fever (redundant).

Recommended: Layered Architecture

Layer 1 — Operations (You Own This)
ROLLER or Mindbody — Memberships, day passes, capacity management, POS, guest data. This is YOUR system.
Layer 2 — Distribution (Fever)
Fever sells INTO Layer 1 capacity. Drives discovery with 200M+ users. GA ticketing only. Founding memberships stay on Layer 1.
Layer 3 — Events (Flexible)
Special programming runs through Layer 1 system or Fever depending on scale. No need for a third platform.
Critical Contract Point with Fever

Submersive MUST retain guest data and email for all Fever-sold tickets. If Fever owns the customer relationship, Submersive becomes a venue-for-hire on its own property. Negotiate co-ownership or full ownership of customer data.

We'd recommend a layered approach — one platform you own for memberships and operations, with Fever as a marketing channel feeding into your capacity. The founding membership relationship should never live inside Fever.
