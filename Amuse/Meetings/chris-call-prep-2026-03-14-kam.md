### 6. Ticketing Platform Options

Chris asked for our perspective. Here's the landscape:

**The core challenge:** Submersive has 4 distinct booking needs that almost no single platform handles:

| Need | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Founding Memberships** | Recurring billing, member perks, priority access — CRM ownership is critical |
| **Day Passes (Timed Entry)** | Capacity-managed sessions, walk-up + online, dynamic pricing |
| **Events & Programming** | Special events, collaborations, sound baths, DJ nights |
| **Discovery & Marketing** | Reaching new audiences at scale |

**Platform comparison:**

| Platform | Best For | Memberships | Timed Entry | Capacity Mgmt | Marketing Reach | Pricing |
|----------|----------|-------------|-------------|---------------|----------------|---------|
| **Fever** | Discovery + marketing + GA ticketing | No | Yes | Session-based | 200M+ users | Commission (est. 15-30%) |
| **Tock** | Prepaid reservations (hospitality) | No | Yes (experiences) | Per-experience | None | $99-899/mo + 2-3% |
| **Mindbody** | Wellness/fitness booking | Yes | Yes (appointments) | Staff/room scheduling | Consumer marketplace | $99-399+/mo |
| **ROLLER** | Attractions + timed entry + memberships | Yes | Yes | Real-time zones | None | By quote |
| **FareHarbor** | Activities/experiences | Yes (basic) | Yes | Per-slot | Booking.com distribution | Customer-facing fee (~6%) |

**Our recommendation: Layered approach**

| Layer | Purpose | Platform | Why |
|-------|---------|----------|-----|
| **Layer 1: Operations** | Memberships, day passes, capacity, POS | **ROLLER** or **Mindbody** | Owns the guest relationship. Handles memberships + timed entry + capacity across zones. ROLLER is purpose-built for attractions; Mindbody if wellness framing matters more. |
| **Layer 2: Distribution** | Marketing + general admission sales | **Fever** | Fever sells INTO your Layer 1 capacity. Drives discovery with 200M+ user base. But they handle GA ticketing only — founding memberships stay on Layer 1. |
| **Layer 3: Events** | Special programming | Layer 1 system or Fever | If events are frequent, handle natively. Fever can market special events through their platform. |

**Critical contract point with Fever:** Submersive must retain guest data and email for ALL Fever-sold tickets. Negotiate co-ownership or full ownership of customer data. If Fever owns the customer relationship, Submersive becomes a venue-for-hire on its own property.

**Platforms NOT recommended as primary:**
- **Tock** — restaurant DNA, no memberships, no venue-scale capacity
- **Eventbrite** — mass-market brand undermines premium positioning, high fees (~10-14%)
- **DICE** — now owned by Fever (acquired June 2025), redundant if already partnering with Fever

**Talking point:** _"We'd recommend a layered approach — one platform you own for memberships and operations, with Fever as a marketing/distribution channel feeding into your capacity. The founding membership relationship should never live inside Fever."_
